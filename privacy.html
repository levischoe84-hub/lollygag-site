Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the Lollygag dating app ("Lollygag", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. Lollygag is published on Google Play by the developer JLLollyGag. Questions: lollygagsupport@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect what a dating app needs to work and keep people safe. Your profile is shown to other members. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads and we don't share your data with advertisers. You can delete your account and data anytime from the app.

Who can use Lollygag

Lollygag is only for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it. Report suspected underage users in the app or by email.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider; we never see them).
  • Profile: first and last name, birth date (to confirm you're 18+ and show your age), gender, who you're looking for, city, bio, likes and dislikes, and photos. Other members see your first name, age, city, approximate distance, photos, bio and interests. Your last name, email and exact birth date are not shown to other members.
  • Activity: swipes and likes, matches, chat messages, ratings you give (for example attraction and date ratings), Admirer guesses, Dream messages, Blind Date and Thursday Date Night entries, and your Discover filters (age range and distance).
  • Safety: reports and blocks you make, and reports made about you.
  • Support: anything you send us by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, your device's location, used to set your city and show approximate distance between members. We store a location point for your profile; other members only see a rounded distance in miles, never your exact location. You can deny location permission and still use the app with your city.
  • Device and notifications: a push notification token for your device and your time zone, so we can send match, message and Date Night notifications at the right time.
  • Purchases: if you buy Lollygag Plus, Google Play handles payment. We receive and store the purchase token, which plan you bought, its status and when it renews or ends. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and errors) for security and troubleshooting.

Camera, microphone and video dates

Camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo for your profile or join a video date (such as Thursday Date Night), and only with your permission. Video and audio go directly between the two people on the call when possible, are encrypted in transit and are not recorded or stored by us. Our servers only pass the connection setup messages needed to start the call. If a direct connection isn't possible, the encrypted stream may pass through a relay server without being stored.

How we use information

  • To run the app: create your account, show your profile, suggest people who fit your preferences and filters, deliver matches, messages and notifications, and run features like Admirer, Blind Dates and Date Night.
  • To provide Lollygag Plus and confirm purchases with Google Play.
  • To keep people safe: check uploaded photos for nudity and violence, filter blocked words in messages and bios, review reports, and remove accounts that break our rules.
  • To fix problems, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law.

Safety and moderation

  • Profile photos are automatically checked by Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch when uploaded. Photos very likely to contain nudity or graphic violence are rejected and deleted; borderline photos may be reviewed by our team.
  • Messages, bios and Dream messages are checked against a list of blocked words.
  • When someone reports you, or you report someone, our team may review the reported profile, photos and the messages in the reported conversation to decide what to do. We don't read other conversations.
  • Accounts with serious or repeated reports may be hidden, warned or banned.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • With other members, as described above (your public profile, and messages with your matches).
  • With service providers that run the app for us and may only use the data to provide their service:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and servers (United States).
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
    • Google Cloud Vision: checking uploaded photos.
    • Google Play: payments for Lollygag Plus.
    • Google STUN servers: helping video calls connect (they see network addresses, not call content).
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the safety of our members or the public.
  • Business transfer: if Lollygag is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with access controls so members can only reach what they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if required by law after a breach.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, matches, messages and other activity are deleted immediately, and server backups are overwritten within about 30 days. If an account was reported or banned, a minimal safety record may be kept for up to 1 year to prevent repeat abuse. See how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, photos and filters anytime in the app.
  • Turn off location, camera, microphone or notification permissions in your phone settings.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile or chat.
  • Delete your account in the app (You → Delete account) or by emailing us.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it by emailing lollygagsupport@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor requests as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
