Delete your Lollygag account

Lollygag is made by the Lollygag team (developer name on Google Play: JLLollyGag).

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Lollygag and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete forever to confirm.

Can't open the app? Ask us

Email lollygagsupport@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Lollygag account. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Your profile: name, birthday, gender, who you're looking for, city, location, bio, interests and photos
  • Your swipes, likes, matches, chat messages, ratings, Blind Date and Date Night history, notifications and filter settings
  • Your device notification tokens and your record of Plus purchases

This happens immediately and can't be undone.

What may be kept

  • Safety records: if you were reported or banned for breaking our rules, a minimal record (for example the reason and date) may be kept for up to 1 year to keep people from returning to harm others.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps its own records of payments under its policies. We don't store your card details.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in encrypted server backups for up to 30 days before it's overwritten.

Have Lollygag Plus?

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in the Play Store: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Lollygag Plus → Cancel.
