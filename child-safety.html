Child safety standards

Effective November 2, 2026

Lollygag is a dating app for adults 18 and older. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any sexualization of minors.

Our rules

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account. Birth dates are checked at sign-up, and accounts that appear to belong to minors are removed.
  • Posting, sharing, requesting or linking to any content that sexualizes or exploits minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Any attempt to contact, groom or arrange to meet a minor is strictly prohibited.

How we prevent and detect it

  • Every uploaded photo is automatically scanned, and photos likely to contain nudity are blocked.
  • Messages and profile text are checked against a list of blocked terms.
  • Anyone can report a profile or conversation in the app, with dedicated "Might be under 18" and "Threats, or I feel unsafe" report options. These reports immediately hide the reported profile while our team reviews it.
  • Members can block anyone at any time.

How we respond

  • Accounts involved in CSAE are permanently banned and the content is removed.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.
  • We preserve relevant information when required for legal investigations.

How to report

In the app, open the profile or chat, tap the menu and choose Report. You can also email our child safety contact at lollygagsupport@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement (911 in the US). You can also report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org.
